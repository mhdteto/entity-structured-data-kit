**How to start a reading journal**

By Alex Rowan · Published by Cedar Notes · October 20, 2026 · English

A short introduction to recording observations about books.

Choose a notebook or plain text file. After each reading session, record the title, the date and one observation in your own words. Review a few entries later to see which ideas remain useful to you.
